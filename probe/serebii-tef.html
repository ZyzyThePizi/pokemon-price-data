Temporal Forces


Release Date: March 22nd 2024
Amount of Cards: 218 (162 Normal, 56 Secret)

This set is the fifth Pokémon Scarlet & Violet TCG set and continues the inclusion of Paradox Pokémon, with a focus on Raging Bolt, Gouging Fire, Walkig Wake, Iron Crown, Iron Boulder and Iron Leaves. It also reintroduces ACE SPEC cards, a special kind of Trainer card where you can only have 1 ACE SPEC in your deck at any one time
